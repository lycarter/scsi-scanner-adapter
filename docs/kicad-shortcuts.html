KiCad 9 PCB Editor shortcuts

Taken from your own ~/Library/Preferences/kicad/9.0/user.hotkeys (macOS, so Ctrl is ⌘). The full list in KiCad is ⌘+F1.

A typical trace

  1. X start
  2. click pad
  3. W width
  4. / flip the corner
  5. click to commit
  6. V via, change layer
  7. F auto-finish or click pad
  8. Esc exit

Start routing

XRoute single track
6Route differential pairNets must be named as a pair, like FOO_P/FOO_N or FOO+/FOO-
⇧+XRoute from the selected item
⇧+ERoute from the selected item's other end
⌘+EContinue routing from the end of a track
⇧+FAutoroute the selected itemsA simple point-to-point router, not a full autorouter

While routing in route

/Switch posture: 45° first or straight first
⌘+/Switch corner style: mitred, rounded (arc), or 90°
⇧+SpaceToggle the 45° angle constraint
⌫Undo the last segment
FTry to finish the route to the nearest targetOutside routing, F flips the item to the other side
EndFinish here (leaves a dangling end)
EscCancel the current trace. Press it again to leave the tool.
click / ⏎Commit the segments up to the cursor

LLock or unlock (the router won't shove locked tracks)

These routing actions have no key by default. The suggested keys are free in your current setup. Bind them in Preferences → Hotkeys (⌘+,) by searching for the action name.

Suggested

F5Switch to Inner Layer 1 Action: layerInner1
F6Switch to Inner Layer 2 Action: layerInner2. Mac F-keys may need fn.
YCycle router mode: highlight, shove, walkaround Action: CycleRouterMode
IRun DRC Action: runDRC
JBreak a track at the cursor Action: BreakTrack

Other unbound actions

—Show or hide the ratsnest showRatsnest
—Select every track in a net SelectNet
—Clean up tracks and vias cleanupTracksAndVias
—Show zones as fill or outline zoneDisplayToggle
—Choose the layer pair SelectLayerPair

Some keys change meaning while the router is active. V, F, ⌫, ⌘+V and ⌘+E are the main ones; their cards say which meaning applies when. Source: KiCad 9.0 user.hotkeys, read 2026-10-02. If you rebind keys, re-check against ⌘+F1.
